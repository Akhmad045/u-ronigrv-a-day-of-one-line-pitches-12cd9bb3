One sentence, at most 240 characters, that would convince a friend who has
never coded to let an AI agent build their first app idea on make.fun.

- Referral code: 4g7knchf

## Entry

> You describe your app idea in plain words, an AI agent on make.fun builds a working app from it, and you can try it, tweak it, and share it - no coding needed, just your idea.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This assembles the sentence, asserts it is a single sentence of 1–240
characters, and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
